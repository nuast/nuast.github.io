About This Site

This page is the central hub for NUAST Computer Science students. Its main purpose is to help you find the right repositories, resources, and learning materials hosted in the nuast-dev organisation on GitHub.

Whether you are looking for starter code, coursework templates, reference projects, or external tools, you will find a curated list of links below. Bookmark this page and use it as your starting point.

About NUAST Computer Science

NUAST (Nottingham University Academy of Science and Technology) is a school that specialises in science and technology education. Our Computer Science department aims to provide students with both the theoretical knowledge and practical skills needed for the digital world.

We use GitHub as a central tool for sharing code, managing projects, and building a portfolio of work. The nuast-dev organisation is where all student-facing coding resources, templates, and materials are hosted.

This site is maintained by the NUAST CS department. If you spot a broken link or would like to suggest a resource, please contact your teacher.
